A **pigeon** is a flying neutral mob that lives in [pigeonhole](@envelope:oak_pigeonhole) and interacts with [mailbox](@envelope:mailbox) to deliver mail.\
When a pigeon stays in [the Nether](https://minecraft.wiki/w/The_Nether) for 15 seconds, it will become a [Charred Pigeon](@envelope:charred_pigeon).

## Variants and Spawning
There are currently 4 natural pigeon variants in the mod:

- <ModAsset location="envelope:pigeon/pigeon_gray" width={54} /> Gray
- <ModAsset location="envelope:pigeon/pigeon_brown" width={54} /> Brown
- <ModAsset location="envelope:pigeon/pigeon_white" width={54} /> White
- <ModAsset location="envelope:pigeon/pigeon_passenger" width={54} /> Passenger

And 2 special variants:
- <ModAsset location="envelope:pigeon/pigeon_charred" width={54} /> Charred
- <ModAsset location="envelope:pigeon/pigeon_archimedes" width={54} /> Archimedes

**Gray**, **Brown** and **White** are what's known as "common variants". They spawn in mountainous biomes and in a village, with following distribution:
 - **Gray**: 62.5%
 - **Brown**: 31.25%
 - **White**: 6.25%

**Passenger** variant spawns in Old Growth biomes (birch, pine and spruce). They **do not** spawn in a village.

<Callout variant="info">Village spawning works similar to how cats spawn in vanilla.</Callout>

A pigeon will have a **Charred** variant when a [Charred Pigeon](@envelope:charred_pigeon) converts into regular pigeon.

**Archimedes** has 1 in 20 chance to spawn when player blows up a *zombie, pillager, piglin or wandering trader*, granting a hidden advancement.

## Behavior

### Breeding

When pigeons are given seeds, they enter love mode. Breeding creates a baby pigeon, and the parents cannot breed again for 5 minutes.
Variant of the baby is chosen randomly from one of the parents with a *50%/50%* chance.

If parent with a special variant is chosen - variant from common pool would be selected instead.</Callout>

### Eating
Pigeons look for seed items dropped on the ground and eat them. They can eat a lot of seeds, so be careful.\
Nitwit villagers will occasionally throw some seeds for nearby pigeons.
