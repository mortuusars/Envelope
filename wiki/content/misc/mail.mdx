**Mail** is an item that's being carried by a courier during [delivery](@envelope:delivery).\
Delivered mail will show a sender [address](address) in item's tooltip.

## Mailable
To apply an [Address Tag](@envelope:address_tag) to the item or insert the item into
[Mailbox](@envelope:mailbox) sending slot, it needs to have a `#envelope:mailable` tag.
